1. Wireless Auto-Discovery – Phone automatically finds the laptop on the local network (mDNS/Bonjour) and connects without typing an IP address.

3. Smart Reconnect (Reboot Fix) – If the phone reboots, the companion app auto-reconnects to the laptop within 10 seconds. No USB cable needed ever again.

4. Screen Mirroring (scrcpy) – Full, low-latency screen mirroring with keyboard and mouse control.

5. Screen-Off Battery Saver – A toggle to turn off the Redmi's physical screen while the laptop mirror stays active. Saves massive battery.

6. Drag and Drop File Transfer – Drag an APK, MP3, or PDF from your laptop desktop onto the phone's mirror window and it instantly copies to the phone.

7. Real-Time Notification Mirror – See WhatsApp, SMS, and M-Pesa popups on your laptop screen. Click them to dismiss on the phone.

8. Quick Reply from Laptop – Type a reply in the notification popup on your laptop, hit Enter, and it sends via WhatsApp/Telegram/SMS natively.

9. Bi-Directional Clipboard – Copy on phone → Paste (Ctrl+V) on laptop. Copy on laptop (Ctrl+C) → Paste instantly on phone.

10. File Explorer (Native) – Browse your Redmi's internal storage and SD card like a normal Windows/Linux folder. Sort by size, date, etc.

11. Smart Media Offloader – Auto-compresses photos/videos older than 30 days to HEIC/HEVC (saves 40% space) and moves originals to a folder on your laptop.

12. Batch APK Installer – Drag 10 APK files onto the tool and install them all on your Redmi simultaneously.

13. APK Extractor – Right-click an app on the phone list and select "Extract APK" to save it to your laptop.

14. Folder Sync (Real-Time) – Select a folder on your PC and a folder on your phone. Edit a text file on your PC and it updates on the phone instantly. Detects single-character changes.

15. Cross-Network File Transfer – Send large files (GBs) between devices on different networks. Uses chunked transfer + resume capability.

16. MIUI Debloat Guardian (GUI) – A color-coded list of 200+ Xiaomi packages. Check the box to uninstall bloatware (GetApps, MSA, Games, etc.). One-click Restore if an update breaks something.

17. Battery Health Dashboard – Shows real-time battery temperature, voltage, charge cycles, and estimated health %. Alerts you if the phone is overheating.

18. Find My Phone (Offline) – A big red button that makes your Redmi ring at MAX volume for 30 seconds, even if it's on silent/vibrate.

19. Mobile Data Tether Toggle – One-click button to enable USB/Wi-Fi tethering so your laptop uses your Redmi's mobile data.

20. SMS and Call Log Archiver – One-click backup of all SMS and call history to a CSV/PDF file stored on your laptop.

21. M-Pesa Statement Tracker – Automatically scans SMS for M-Pesa keywords. Creates a local database on your laptop with date, amount, sender/recipient, and type (Sent/Received).

22. Low-Data Mode – Limits background sync and file transfers to occur ONLY when your laptop is plugged into power.

23. Safaricom/Airtel USSD Shortcut – A button on the desktop that sends a USSD code (e.g., *544#) to the phone and displays the balance response directly on your laptop screen.

24. Network Signal Logger – Logs your Redmi's cellular signal strength over time. Helps you identify dead zones.

25. Offline Maps Sync – Downloads offline map data from your phone to your laptop (or vice versa) so you don't burn data navigating.

26. Remote Camera View – Use your Redmi's camera as a wireless webcam for your laptop. Useful for Zoom/Google Meet when your laptop webcam is broken.

27. Tasker / Macro Integration – Allows you to trigger phone tasks from your laptop (e.g., send a message, open an app, toggle Bluetooth) via custom hotkeys.

28. Scheduled Maintenance Mode – Runs a cleaning routine every night at 2 AM: clears app cache, compresses photos, backs up new files, and reboots the phone.

29. WhatsApp/Telegram Database Backup – Automatically detects WhatsApp databases on the phone, compresses them, and saves them to your laptop daily.

30. Universal Clipboard History – Saves the last 50 copied items (text/images) from both your phone and laptop into a searchable history panel.

31. Multi-Device Support – Connect 2 or 3 Android phones simultaneously and switch between them in the sidebar.

32. Screen Recorder – Records your phone's screen directly to your laptop's hard drive.

33. Internet Screen Sharing (PC-to-PC) – Share your laptop/PC screen with another LiteLink user over the internet. View and control remotely.

34. Device Discovery (Cloud) – See all your registered devices (Phone, PC, Laptop) in a dashboard, regardless of network.

35. Remote Clipboard (Internet) – Copy text on your PC in Nairobi → Paste on your laptop in Mombasa. Works across networks.
